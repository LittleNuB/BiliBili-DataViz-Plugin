# Keep conversations bounded and save selected content

Question-and-answer sessions remain capacity-bounded local history rather than automatically becoming permanent knowledge. Users may explicitly preserve a sourced selection through the native context-menu action or save one validated question-and-answer turn through its secondary menu; either operation creates an independent durable knowledge asset, so later session deletion or least-recently-used cleanup cannot remove it. A cross-video turn remains one multi-source asset rather than being split or duplicated per cited video.
