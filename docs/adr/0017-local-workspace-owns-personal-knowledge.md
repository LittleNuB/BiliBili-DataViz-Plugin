# Local workspace owns personal knowledge

The personal video knowledge base belongs to the extension's local browser-profile workspace, while each connected Bilibili account contributes separately scoped favorite and watch-later relationships. This keeps one person's durable notes and saved knowledge unified across their accounts without allowing one account's sync or removal to mutate another account's relationships; a newly detected account requires confirmation before import, and account identifiers remain local rather than entering AI payloads.
