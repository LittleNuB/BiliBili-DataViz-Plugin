# Send only selected personal knowledge to AI

Under a separate knowledge-base AI authorization, Bili-Bill may send the complete primary text of selected videos or parts together with only the personal knowledge assets retrieved as directly relevant to the submitted question. Personal notes remain labeled as personal knowledge and cannot stand in for video evidence without a current exact citation; the full knowledge space, relationship lists, account identifiers, unrelated notes, temporary caches, and changed historical evidence remain outside the request.
