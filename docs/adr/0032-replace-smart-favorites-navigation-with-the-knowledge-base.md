# Replace Smart Favorites navigation with the knowledge base

The Dashboard sidebar replaces the top-level Smart Favorites destination with Knowledge Base so users do not face two overlapping products for preserving and finding video-related material. Existing favorite synchronization, folder organization, classification, and index diagnostics move under the knowledge base's 收藏来源 area without deleting or promoting synchronized records to durable knowledge. Global knowledge search becomes the single cross-layer search surface, the legacy Smart Favorites route redirects to 收藏来源, and Dynamic Bill and Video Blind Box remain independent top-level views because their purposes are interest rebalancing and exploration rather than knowledge maintenance.
