# Keep Smart Favorite indexes as rebuildable source classification

Existing `smartFavoriteIndex` data remains rebuildable 收藏分类 under 收藏来源 rather than being migrated into personal notes, knowledge tags, evidence, or managed full text. Its category path, generated summary, keywords, aliases, and searchable text may provide a low-priority local retrieval signal labeled “收藏分类”, but cannot support factual AI claims, enter knowledge backup, or outrank personal knowledge and video text. Clearing or rebuilding this derived index therefore cannot mutate canonical videos or durable knowledge.
