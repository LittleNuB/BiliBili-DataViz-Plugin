# Separate editable organization from read-only evidence

Every durable knowledge asset separates editable user organization from a read-only evidence layer. Titles, personal notes, local knowledge tags, and pure-note bodies may change, while captured subtitle or transcript wording, preserved answer wording, source videos and parts, time ranges, and citation relationships cannot be edited in place. Correcting a genuinely wrong mapping requires an explicit source-relocation operation. This preserves the user's ability to maintain personal understanding without allowing ordinary editing to rewrite the historical material that supported it.
