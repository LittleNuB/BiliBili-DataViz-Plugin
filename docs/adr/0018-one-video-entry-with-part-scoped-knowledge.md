# One video entry with part-scoped knowledge

The knowledge base represents one Bilibili submission as one top-level video entry, while its parts remain subordinate scopes for evidence, notes, and navigation. This avoids duplicating large courses and compilations across the library without weakening provenance: every local claim, citation, and timestamp still binds to the exact part that supplied it, and unavailable or changed parts leave durable personal knowledge preserved with an honest status.
