# Preserve knowledge without reusing changed evidence

When a video's part structure or exact text source changes, Bili-Bill preserves user-authored work and explicitly saved historical results but removes the affected source-derived item's eligibility to support new claims. This avoids both destructive cleanup and silent evidence drift: updating or regenerating remains user-initiated, while temporary unsaved caches may still be replaced or evicted under bounded storage rules.
