# Clear the whole knowledge space without clearing source relationships

“清空知识库” atomically removes every durable knowledge asset, evidence snapshot, user edit, managed full-text material, imported conflict version, and derived index entry after showing category counts, storage size, a backup action, and a typed confirmation. It does not delete ordinary sessions, temporary active-video source caches, synchronized favorite or watch-later relationships, account source relationships, settings, credentials, or unrelated module data. Retained source relationships may recreate lightweight video entries after synchronization, but cannot restore deleted personal knowledge. Per-video storage management may remove one complete text without deleting related knowledge assets or evidence snapshots, while the broader all-local-data operation remains separately available.
