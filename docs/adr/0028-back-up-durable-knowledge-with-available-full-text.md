# Back up durable knowledge with available full text

Bili-Bill exports one local, versioned compressed backup package. Every backup contains all durable knowledge assets, user edits, video and part links, multi-source relationships, and evidence snapshots. By default it also contains every managed full-text material in the local knowledge space, while the user may explicitly create a lightweight backup without those complete texts. Temporary source caches for active videos outside the knowledge space remain excluded. Export reports estimated size and available or missing full-text coverage instead of implying completeness. Audio, transcription models, ordinary conversation history, rebuildable indexes and generated caches, synchronized account data, AI configuration, credentials, and login state remain outside the package. Restored complete texts are immediately readable and searchable, but cannot support new current factual claims until their video, exact part, and source state are revalidated.
